How do room packs work?

1. Insert a pack in one click (`Alt+R`)

From the estimate builder, press Alt+R or click the button «⚡ Room Pack» on the sticky bar to insert any room instantly.

2. Smart proportional scaling

Enter the room’s actual floor area (e.g. 14 m² for a bathroom). The system scales the tile and floor-levelling quantities to match while keeping fixture quantities fixed.

3. Save your own sections

Did you design a stunning kitchen you want to estimate again? Click «💾 Save pack» in the header of that section and save it to this library.
