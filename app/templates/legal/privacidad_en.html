Privacy policy

Version 1.2 · In force since September 8, 2026

In short: we store the minimum needed for the service to work; we never see your passwords (Supabase Auth manages them); your commercial data and the assistant conversations are not sold, not shared with advertisers and not used to train models; you can request a copy or their deletion.

1. Controller

The data controller is {{ titular_legal }}, operator of {{ product_name }}. Contact: {{ email_soporte }}.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your data.
  • We do not use your commercial data for advertising or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not use your content or the assistant conversations to train models or for a purpose other than providing and improving the service.
  • If you request a generative response, we send the necessary history and indispensable context to the AI provider indicated below. Local tools can answer without that sending.
  • We do not install third-party trackers for advertising purposes. The desktop version sends no telemetry.

These providers may host servers outside your country. We choose services with industry-standard security measures and limit the data each one receives to what is indispensable for its function.

5. Isolation between organizations

Each organization is a separate space: isolation is applied at the data layer (mandatory filter by organization in every query and row-level security policies in PostgreSQL) and in file access. A member of one organization cannot see another's data. The only deliberate exception is a proposal shared by your own organization: whoever holds its secret link can view only that version and its PDF until the link expires or is revoked.

6. Cookies

We use only technical session cookies (HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite): they identify your session and your active organization. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Without these cookies the service cannot work, so they do not require additional consent.

7. Retention

  • Your organization's data is retained while the account is active.
  • Assistant conversations are retained for 365 days from the last turn. An automatic job deletes them when that period expires; the panel stops showing them before the purge.
  • If you cancel, data remains available for export for a reasonable period (minimum 30 days) and may then be deleted.
  • You can request earlier access, export or deletion of your conversations by writing to support. Managed backups rotate and deletions propagate to them with the cadence of that rotation.

8. Your rights

You can exercise at any time, by writing to {{ email_soporte }}:

  • Access and portability: obtain a copy of your data (the built-in exports already cover most of it).
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data (you can usually do it yourself from the application).
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and of your organization's data (if the organization has more members, the request must come from its owner). You can also request deletion of a specific conversation.

We respond within a maximum of 30 days.

9. Security

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS) throughout the service.
  • Passwords managed exclusively by Supabase Auth; recovery via single-use link.
  • Sessions with HttpOnly cookies not accessible from JavaScript, CSRF protection and strict content security policy (CSP).
  • Private file storage, served only with membership authorization.
  • Database roles without administrative privileges and row-level isolation verified by automated tests.

If a security incident affecting your data were to occur, we will notify you without undue delay together with the measures taken.

10. Minors

{{ product_name }} is a professional tool for companies and is not aimed at minors.

11. Changes to this policy

If we substantially change this policy we will announce it by email or within the application with reasonable notice. The current version will always be published on this page.
