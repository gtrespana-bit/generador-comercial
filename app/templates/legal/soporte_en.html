Support terms

Version 1.0 · In force since August 15, 2026

1. Channel

The support channel is the email {{ email_soporte }}. During the early access stage it is also the channel for suggestions and for arranging assisted configuration sessions.

2. What support includes

  • Help configuring your company, catalog and data import.
  • Answers to questions about any feature.
  • Investigation and correction of product bugs.
  • Recovery from problems accessing your account or organization.

3. What it does not include

  • Tax, legal or pricing advice for your business.
  • Manual loading of your full catalog (import from Excel and from the local installation exists; we can guide you to use it).
  • Custom feature development, except under a separate written agreement.
  • Hardware, operating system or network support.

4. Hours and indicative times

  • Support hours: business days, 9:00–18:00, in your country's time zone (Caracas UTC−4 · Bogotá/Lima UTC−5 · Mexico City UTC−6). We reply during the customer's business hours.
  • First response: normally the same business day; maximum 2 business days.
  • Errors that prevent work (you cannot access or generate documents): top priority; we start investigating as soon as the report is received within support hours.

During the early access stage these times are effort commitments, not contractual guarantees with penalties.

5. How to report a bug

To resolve it quickly, include in your email:

  1. What you were trying to do and what you expected to happen.
  2. What happened instead (exact error message, if any).
  3. Steps to reproduce, if you know them.
  4. Screenshot or affected PDF, without personal data of your clients if you can avoid it.
  5. Approximate date and time, and whether you use the web or the desktop app.

6. Maintenance and notices

Scheduled maintenance that involves unavailability is announced in advance by email or within the application. Relevant incidents are communicated with their resolution and, when appropriate, with the measures to prevent recurrence.
