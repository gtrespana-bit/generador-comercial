About the product

What exactly is {{ product_name }}?

A web application for construction and remodeling estimates. You store (or use) a catalog of line items with costs and breakdowns, build estimates by sections, see margin and times internally, and deliver a professional PDF by WhatsApp or email. After the yes, it turns the estimate into a project, records scope changes and controls collections. It is not a loose document generator: it is the commercial flow of the job.

The full guide is at How it works.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser and your data stays on the server, isolated by organization. There is a desktop version only as compatibility for those who already had it: you can import that local installation to the web from Settings → Import installation. New pilots go through the web.

Does it fit my type of work?

It is designed for small and medium construction companies, remodelers, contractors and installers in Latin America and Spain. If you prepare between 2 and 50 estimates a month and today you do them in Word or Excel, it fits. It brings terminology and VAT for Venezuela, Colombia, Mexico, Ecuador, Peru and Spain, and local currency, EUR or USD.

Can I see a sample estimate?

Yes. There is a complete one in PDF from the product page. All its data is fictional. The landing itself shows a bathroom remodel estimate with cost, profit and hours, converted to your country's currency.

Does the client have to register?

No. You send the PDF by WhatsApp or email as you already do. The client reviews the document and replies. There is an optional private link if you want them to sign on screen, but it is not required and does not replace your relationship with them.

Catalog, costs and resources

What catalog is included?

{{ catalogo().partidas_txt }} decomposed line items and {{ catalogo().recursos_txt }} resource prices, organized in {{ catalogo().capitulos }} sections. {{ catalogo().lineas_descomp_txt }} unit price analysis lines (materials, labor, equipment and complementary). Can I bring my catalog from Excel?

Yes. Import from Excel (.xlsx), including breakdowns with a resource matrix, productivity and formulas, without losing rows. You can also paste TSV copied from a sheet, or import a previous local installation of {{ product_name }}.

If I change the price of cement, do the line items update?

Yes. Resources are connected in cascade: resource → line items that use it → estimates in editing. Documents already sent are not rewritten: each version is frozen.

What is a room pack?

A complete section (bathroom, kitchen, floors…) inserted with Alt+R and scaled to the real m². {{ catalogo().packs or 6 }} packs included, and you can save your own from any assembled section.

Estimates, margin and delivery

Does it calculate profit margin?

Yes, if you enter the internal cost of each line item. It compares cost with selling price and shows profit and margin in % and in money: per line item, per section, for the job and for the products separately. It warns when a line item has low margin or an unreviewed price. The client does not see those numbers in the PDF.

Does it calculate construction time?

Yes, internally. With labor productivity it estimates man-hours by role and duration in working days. You can assign hours by hand. That information never goes to the client's PDF: it is so you can promise schedules you can actually meet, not so they claim printed hours.

What does the PDF include?

Your logo, sections, line items, measurements, products with photo, terms, warranties, totals and VAT, and optionally cover, signatures and annexes merged as real pages. Configurable styles. It does not include internal costs, margin or crew hours.

Can I send via WhatsApp?

Yes. The button opens WhatsApp with the message already drafted (client, number, total, validity). You can also send it by email from the app, with the PDF attached.

What if the client asks for changes?

You edit the estimate. A new version is created (the previous one is sealed) and CotizaT builds a summary to copy: previous total, new total and main changes. You resend through the same channel.

Data, import and control

Can I export my data and leave?

Yes, and without asking permission. The data you enter is yours. You export to Excel and PDF whenever you want, with full backup. If you end the service you have a window of at least 30 days to export it, according to the terms of service.

Can other companies see my prices?

No. Each organization is isolated in the database itself, not only on screen. Images and attachments are served after permission checks, never from a public address. A proposal link, if you create one, shows only that version until it expires or you revoke it.

Who can enter my organization?

Only those you invite, with the role you assign: read-only (view and download), member (create and edit) or administrator (also manage the team). Invitations expire and can only be used once, and whoever accepts must have the same confirmed email.

In which countries and currencies does it work?

Designed for Latin America and Spain. When you register you choose Venezuela, Colombia, Mexico, Ecuador, Peru or Spain (VAT, tax ID and vocabulary). You quote in USD, EUR or your local currency; each estimate freezes its rate. There are 20 supported ISO currencies. Without a verified rate we do not invent a conversion.

Invoicing and tax scope

Does {{ product_name }} issue tax invoices?

No. The documents it generates —estimates and billing documents— are commercial documents. They do not replace a tax invoice issued according to the regulation that applies to you, and the documents themselves warn of this. For tax invoicing use the means required by your country and consult your advisor.

Does it calculate taxes automatically?

You can configure the taxes you apply (VAT, withholding, exempt operation) and {{ product_name }} calculates them in the document, but the responsibility for which type applies to each concept is yours or your advisor's: we do not provide tax advice.

By country

Is there a CotizaT page for my country?

Yes. Each launch market has its own URL, vocabulary, VAT and tax ID. It is not the same page with the country name changed:

  • Venezuela — USD or Bs, RIF, 16 % VAT, concreto and friso. Also software and APU.
  • Colombia — COP, NIT, 19 % VAT, pañete and APU. Also unit price analysis and remodeling.
  • Mexico — MXN, RFC, 16 % VAT, aplanado and plafón. Also quoting a remodel.
  • Peru — soles, RUC, 18 % IGV, metrados and tarrajeo. Also APU and metrados.
  • Ecuador — USD, RUC, 15 % VAT, enlucido and tumbado. Also APU in dollars.

The guides (method, not the panel) are at construction estimate, what an APU is and estimating a remodel.

Does it issue DIAN, CFDI, SUNAT or SRI invoices?

No. In no country. The estimate is a commercial document. The electronic invoice of Colombia (DIAN), the CFDI of Mexico, the SUNAT receipt of Peru or the SRI invoice in Ecuador are issued with the means required by your jurisdiction.
