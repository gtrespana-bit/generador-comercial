How renewals and switches work

  • Renewing never eats days: the new period always chains after the access you already have, whether you buy today or on the last day.
  • Switching between monthly and annual means buying the other period: it starts when the current one ends, with the exact dates shown on each card.
  • With a card (Stripe) renewal is automatic; turn it off above any time and access continues through the last paid day.
  • With a manual payment there are no automatic charges: if you do not renew, access ends on the expiry date and your data is kept for when you come back.
  • Problem with a charge? Write to us from Support with the purchase date.
