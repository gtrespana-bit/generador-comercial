The essentials, without complicating it

CotizaT does not aim to replace your relationship with the client. It helps you prepare a clear PDF, protect your margin and respond quickly when they ask for changes.

1

Set up your company

Logo, tax details, currency, VAT, terms and texts that will appear in the PDF.

Go to settings
2

Review the catalog

Check catalog health: prices, costs, times and low margins before quoting.

View catalog
3

Create client and estimate

Build sections and line items from catalog, pack, Excel, BC3 (FIEBDC-3) or manually. If you have a plan, upload it and measure on top.

New client
4

Review before sending

Use estimate health to detect zero price, zero quantity, missing cost or low margin.

View estimates
5

Download and send PDF

Send the PDF by WhatsApp or email as you normally work. The client reviews and contacts you.

Open estimates
6

If they ask for changes, modify and resend

When you change a versioned estimate, CotizaT shows what changed and generates a summary to copy.

Resend changes

Checklist before the first real estimate

  • ✓ Company and logo configured.
  • ✓ Currency, VAT and rate reviewed.
  • ✓ Catalog without critical alerts.
  • ✓ Client with phone or email.
  • ✓ Estimate with complete quantities and prices.
  • ✓ Margin reviewed before sending.
  • ✓ PDF opened once before sharing.

What to look at and what not to complicate

Use it for

  • Quoting fast.
  • Knowing if you make money.
  • Preparing a professional PDF.
  • Resending clear changes.
  • Keeping prices and costs healthy.

No need

  • Putting the client into a complex system.
  • Showing them costs or margin.
  • Turning every call into an online procedure.
  • Using every feature on day one.
